What is Geriatric Medicine?

Health reviewed as a whole

A geriatrician reviews medical conditions together with memory, mobility, medicines, nutrition, and daily function. The aim is a clearer plan for the older adult and the family — not a rushed visit focused on one symptom alone.

Caring for older adults asks for patience, science, and the time to understand a full life history.

Who may need evaluation

When a geriatric visit may help

These are reasons families often seek a specialist review. They are not a diagnosis. A medical evaluation may be appropriate if you notice one or more of the following.

Repeated falls or problems with balance
Changes in memory or thinking
Unexplained weight loss or reduced appetite
General weakness or difficulty moving
Reduced ability to manage daily activities
Many medicines, or several chronic conditions
